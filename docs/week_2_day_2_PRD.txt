2. Objective

Scientific parameters are calculated (inline or via imports).

Data is fetched from Supabase (e.g., queries to the ingredients table).

Consolidate: Refactor to ensure a single data entry point flows into a single calculation engine.

Cleanup: Deprecate calc.ts and inconsistent fetchers.
